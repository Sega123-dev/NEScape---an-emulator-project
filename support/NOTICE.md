# Important note

This is the official list of all supported ROM files of NEScape NES emulator.

They are separated in multiple files based on what mappers they use(or not).
Emulator is currently at the development stage so support is pretty limited.

Boot estimate: **83% of commercial and homebrew titles**

For the first testing,I am aiming to test NEScape with over 150 commercial games and over 50 homebrew ROMs and test releases.

I will be doing it in batches that are linked to each mapper(probably will be 25 to 30 games per each mapper).

Current mappers supported: 0,1,2,3,4,7(experimental),11.

**No support for mapper extension and different solder boards.**

Emulator usually is going to be tested with EU/USA/World version of ROMs,but I might test with JP ROMs for specific franchises.

Support not related to mappers and memory:
- Input is supported(keyboard only).
- Zapper is not fully supported.
- Grayscale is supported but not really in the scope of importance for the support
- UI is not yet supported
- Monitoring and logs are limited and not supported in the traditional way
- Battery-backed saving not yet supported
- Save states not supported
- Dualshock and other controllers are not supported but they are in the scope

**NOTE:** This list will be updated regularly and could change over time depending on the changes of scope and long-term goals for this project.
